This is my Coptic page. What is here is mostly tools: the things I kept
having to build in order to read, type or find Coptic, tidied up far enough
that somebody else can use them.

Atripe was a town in Upper Egypt across the river from Panopolis, and the
place Shenoute is named after. Very little of it remains. The name seemed
appropriate for a page that is mostly work I no longer have a department to
do.

Everything here is free, finished enough to use, and unfinished enough that
I would like to hear about the parts that break.
