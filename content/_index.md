Atripe was a town in Upper Egypt across the river from Panopolis, and the
place Shenoute is named after. Nothing much is left of it. This site borrows
the name for a smaller thing: a desk where notes about Coptic get written
down so they stop getting lost.

What lives here — short notes on orthography, script, and the manuscripts;
a hand-kept [list of links](@/links/_index.md) to the tools and corpora that
are actually worth a bookmark; and occasional complaints about fonts.

It is one person's notebook, not a reference work. Everything is provisional
and corrections are welcome.
