### Name, place, and time
**The name**
_Mesokemic_ means 'middle Egyptian', from Greek μέσον 'middle' and Coptic ⲕⲏⲙⲉ 'Egypt'.
The foreignisms are chosen to avoid a name collision with the Middle Egyptian stage of the language (2000–1300 BC).
Another name of the dialect is _Oxyrhynchite_ (see below).
The standard siglum for the dialect is _M_.{{ cite(key="Kasser1990") }}

**The place**
may have been [Oxyrhynchus](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Oxyrhynchus&params=28.531_N_30.647_E_type:city_region:EG) (modern البَهنَسَا, _al-Bahnasā_),
judged from its position in the continuum between Fayyumic and Lycopolitan.
The psalter was found at al-Mudil (c. 45 km north of Oxyrhynchus).
Findspots of other major sources remain unknown.

**The time of attestation** are the fourth and fifth centuries.
From the 6th/7th c. onwards, the dialect is replaced by the Sahidic standard.{{ cite(key="Richter2023") }}

### Linguistic features

**The closest dialects** to _M_ are _F_ (Fayyumic), _V_ (Fayyumic without lambdacism), _W_ (crypto-Mesokemic).
_V_ and _W_ each come from single manuscripts (P.Mich. 3520 and 3521, 4th/5th c.) and sit between _F_ and _M_: The resulting north-to-south-continuum is _B_ – _F_ – _V_/_W_ – _M_ – _L_.

**The quintessential features** of Mesokemic can be observed in the sentence(s)
ⲉ̀ⲡϫⲟⲕ ⲉ̀ⲃⲁⲗ ⲡⲉⲯⲁⲗⲙⲟⲥ ⲛ̀ⲇⲁⲩⲉⲓⲇ / ⲁⲩⲱ ϩⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ ⲉ̀ⲡⲁⲧⲟⲃϩ 'This is the end of the psalm of David. And he listened to my prayer.' (Psalm 39), compare Sahidic ⲉⲡϫⲱⲕ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ ⲡⲉⲯⲁⲗⲙⲟⲥ ⲛ̅ⲇⲁⲩⲉⲓⲇ / ⲁⲩⲱ ⲁϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅ ⲉⲡⲁⲧⲱⲃϩ̅.
- ⲟ where other dialects have ⲱ (ϫⲟⲕ, ⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ, ⲧⲟⲃϩ vs. _S_ ϫⲱⲕ, ⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅, ⲧⲱⲃϩ̅); shared with no other dialect.
- ⲁ where _S_ and _B_ have ⲟ (ⲉ̀ⲃⲁⲗ vs. _S_ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ); shared with _F_, _L_, _A_.
- first perfect with ϩⲁ⸗ (ϩⲁ⸗ϥ-ⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲁ⸗ϥ-ⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅); shared with no other dialect.
- supralineation with ϫⲓⲛⲕⲓⲙ similar to Old Bohairic

**Other features** include
- stressed ⲉ where _S_ and _B_ have ⲁ (shared with _F_, _L_, _A_),
- unstressed final -ⲉ where _F_, _B_ have -ⲓ,
- absence of vocalic gemination, as in _B_, _F4_,
- absence of lambdacism, as in all dialects apart from _F_,
- absence of /x/ (ϧ, ⳉ), as in all dialects apart from _B_, _A_.

**Morphological differences from Sahidic** include
- the definite plural article ⲛⲓ- as in ⲛⲓⲇⲓⲕⲁⲓⲟⲥ vs. _S_ ⲛ̅ⲇⲓⲕⲁⲓⲟⲥ,
- the relative 3rd pl. ⲉⲧ⸗ⲥⲉ- as in ⲉⲧⲥⲉⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲉⲧⲟⲩⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the preterite morpheme ⲛⲁ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲛⲉ⸗ as in ⲛⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲛⲉϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the future morpheme ⲛⲉ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲛⲁ⸗ as in ϥⲛⲉⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ϥⲛⲁⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the 1st perfect ϩⲁ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲁ⸗ as in ϩⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲁϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
  the ϩ- might come from the verb ⲟⲩⲟϩ 'to set',{{ cite(key="Allen2020", loc="9.2") }}
- the relative 1st perfect ⲉⲑⲁ⸗ (< ⲉⲧ(ⲉ)ϩⲁ⸗) vs. _S_ ⲛ̅ⲧⲁ⸗ as in ⲉⲑⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲛ̅ⲧⲁϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the 2nd present ⲁ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲉ⸗ as in ⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲉϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the 2nd perfect ⲉϩⲁ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲛ̅ⲧⲁ⸗ as in ⲉϩⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲛ̅ⲧⲁϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the negative optative (TODO name) ⲙ̀ⲡⲉⲣⲧⲉ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲙ̅ⲡⲣ̅ⲧⲣⲉ⸗ as in ⲙ̀ⲡⲉⲣⲧⲉϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲙ̅ⲡⲣ̅ⲧⲣⲉϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the future conjunctive ⲛ̀ⲧⲁⲣⲉ⸗ vs. _S_ ⲧⲁⲣⲉ⸗ as in ⲛ̀ⲧⲁⲣⲉϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲧⲁⲣⲉϥⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- the temporal being replaced by the relative 1st perfect (ⲉⲑⲁϥⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ), as in _B_,
- the conditional ⲁ⸗ϣⲁⲛ- vs. _S_ ⲉ⸗ϣⲁⲛ- as in ⲁϥϣⲁⲛⲥⲟⲧⲉⲙ vs. _S_ ⲉϥϣⲁⲛⲥⲱⲧⲙ̅,
- TODO ⲉⲣ- perfect participial
- qualitatives like ϣⲏⲡⲧ, ϩⲏⲡⲧ for ϣⲟⲡ 'receive' and ϩⲟⲡ 'hide'

### Lexicon

**Common words**
- ⲙⲉⲛ 'with, and' – _S_ ⲙⲛ̅,
- ⲛ̀ϭⲏ (introduces subject) – _S_ ⲛ̅ϭⲓ,
- ⲛⲉ 'see' – _S_ ⲛⲁⲩ,
