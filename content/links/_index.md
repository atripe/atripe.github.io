Kept by hand, checked when something breaks. If a link here has rotted,
say so — the address is at the foot of every page.

## Dictionaries

- [Coptic Dictionary Online](https://coptic-dictionary.org) — the KELLIA
  project's searchable Crum, Černý and Westendorf. The one to use daily.
- [Marcion](https://marcion.sourceforge.net) — an offline reader and
  dictionary. Old-fashioned, still works.

## Texts and corpora

- [Coptic Scriptorium](https://copticscriptorium.org) — annotated,
  searchable Sahidic texts with linguistic markup, freely licensed.
- [CMCL](https://www.cmcl.it) — Corpus dei Manoscritti Copti Letterari,
  the long-running Roman project on the literary manuscripts.

## Manuscripts and places

- [PAThs](https://atlas.paths-erc.eu) — an atlas tying Coptic literary
  production to the places and archaeological sites it came from.
- [Trismegistos](https://www.trismegistos.org) — everything documentary
  from Egypt, cross-referenced within an inch of its life.
- [Claremont Coptic Encyclopedia](https://ccdl.claremont.edu/digital/collection/cce)
  — the full text of the *Coptic Encyclopedia*, free.

## Type

- [Antinoou and friends](https://www.evertype.com/fonts/coptic/) — Michael
  Everson's Coptic faces. Antinoou is the reference face for epigraphy and
  papyrology.
- [New Athena Unicode](https://apagreekkeys.org/NAUdownload.html) — the
  Society for Classical Studies' face, which this page is set in.
