Atripe is the town Shenoute is named after; not much of it is left. He ran
the White Monastery there for most of the fifth century and is the most
prolific author in Coptic. The tagline is only half a joke about hosting: he
is also the first man from Atripe on record as working at a distance, and
the commute is documented.

> 'While he pondered this, suddenly a radiant cloud descended from the heavens. It lifted both him and his disciple into the sky and carried them away. As the bishop's ship reached the open sea, Abba Cyril looked up and saw Apa Shenoute and his disciple being borne aloft in the midst of the cloud. He exclaimed, "Bless us, holy father, you are the new Elijah!" Apa Shenoute answered, "Do not forget me, O my holy father." And with that, the cloud continued on its way and brought him safely home to his monastery.'
